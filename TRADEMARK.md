# Amanah trademark and naming policy

**Version 1.0, 30 July 2026**

The Amanah software is free. The Amanah **name** is not, and this document explains the difference.

The code is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and the specification under CC BY 4.0. Those licences grant
broad rights to use, modify and redistribute the work. Neither licence grants any right to use the
project's name, and this policy governs that separately.

The purpose is not to restrict anyone's freedom to build on this work. It is to make sure that when
someone encounters the name "Amanah" attached to a system that handles clinical follow-up
obligations, they can tell whether it is this project. In a safety-critical context that
distinction matters more than it would elsewhere.

## 1. What the marks are

- The word mark **Amanah**, when used in connection with clinical follow-up, health software, or
  related services.
- Any logo or wordmark subsequently adopted by the project.

Registration status is stated plainly in section 7.

## 2. Uses that need no permission

You may do all of the following freely, with no request to anyone:

- **Refer to the project.** Write about it, review it, criticise it, teach it, cite it.
- **State a factual relationship.** "Built with Amanah", "compatible with Amanah", "imports
  Amanah data", "an Amanah deployment". Truthful descriptive statements are always permitted.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** under the AGPL, keeping the name intact.
- **Run a deployment**, including a public one, and call it what it is.
- **Cite the specification and the concept note** academically. Please use the DOI.
- **Run a community**, meetup, user group or translation effort using the name descriptively, for
  example "Amanah Users, Jakarta".

## 3. Uses that need permission

Ask first for any of these:

- **Distributing a modified version under the Amanah name.** Fork freely, but rename. See section 4.
- **Naming a product, service, company or paid offering** "Amanah" or something confusingly similar
  in this field.
- **Registering a domain, app-store listing, or social account** whose name suggests it is the
  official project.
- **Merchandise** bearing the name or logo.
- **Anything implying endorsement**, partnership, certification or official status.

## 4. Forks and modified versions

You have every right to fork this project. The AGPL guarantees it.

If you distribute a **modified** version, give it a different name. You may and should still state
the lineage: *"NewName, a fork of Amanah"* or *"based on Amanah"*. What you may not do is present
modified software as though it were this project.

This matters more here than in ordinary software. A fork that weakens a safety constraint, for
example one that removes the prohibition on implying an all-clear, could put people at risk while
carrying a name that suggests it did not. Renaming keeps that accountable to whoever made the change.

## 5. Independent implementations of the specification

The **Portable Clinical Obligation** specification is deliberately vendor-neutral and carries a
neutral schema namespace. Implementing it is encouraged and requires no permission.

An independent implementation:

- **may** state that it "implements the Portable Clinical Obligation specification", and may claim
  conformance if it meets the conformance criteria in section 11 of the specification;
- **may not** call itself Amanah, or imply that it is this project's software.

The specification belongs to everyone. The implementation's name does not.

## 6. Uses that will always be refused

Permission will not be granted for use of the name in any of the following, regardless of who asks:

- Software or services that **make diagnostic claims**. The non-interpretive constraint is the
  foundation of this project's safety and regulatory position, and the name will not be attached to
  anything that abandons it.
- Anything implying that Amanah has been **clinically validated, certified or endorsed** by a
  regulator, health system or medical body when it has not.
- Any use suggesting the software **replaces clinical judgement** or constitutes medical advice.

## 7. Registration status

As of this version, "Amanah" is **not a registered trademark** of this project. The name is in use
by unrelated parties in other fields, including finance and healthcare services, and no claim is
made over those uses.

This policy asserts the project's position on use of the name in connection with clinical
follow-up software specifically, and documents that position from the date above. It will be
updated if registration is later obtained.

The absence of registration is not an invitation to appropriate the name. It is a statement of
where the project currently stands.

with the purpose in the opening paragraph are expected to be granted, and granted for free. This
project has no interest in charging anyone for the use of a name.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised. Revisions are versioned and recorded in the repository history.
Permission already granted in writing is not withdrawn by a later revision.
